# T070 Fly the ship: thrust and turn

OPEN AT: The Interloper in space above Efate, the flight screen showing. The flight opens paused: PAUSED - press P to fly; click Pause (or press P) to fly.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/070.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Pause to fly (or press P)
  2. Click the thrust-up arrow (or press Up)
  3. Click the rotate-left arrow twice (or press Left twice)
  4. Click the thrust-down arrow (or press Down)
EVALUATE:
  - Up raises the thrust gauge
  - Left turns the ship on the flight screen
  - Down lowers the thrust again
