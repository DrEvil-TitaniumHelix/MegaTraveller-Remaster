# T077 Jump to another system

OPEN AT: The Interloper standing still in open space in the Efate system (thrust 0: it does not drift onto the Efate dock), Jump-2 and Navigation running, 122 tons of fuel. The flight opens paused: PAUSED - press P to fly; the title reads Flying in the Efate system.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/077.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Pause to fly (or press P), then Jump in the list at the right (or press J)
  2. Read the jump map, then click louzy (or press Enter: louzy is the flashing system)
  3. Click No; click louzy again and click Yes (or press Y)
  4. After the jump film click anywhere (or press a key); then click Jump again (or press J)
EVALUATE:
  - The map says where you are: its title reads Jump map - you are at Efate and efate is green; louzy, uakye and alell are blue (in reach), the other four grey (too far); a line says the flashing system is where you will jump; a Cancel box is drawn
  - The click on louzy asks: Jump from Efate to Louzy? Uses 61 tons of fuel, takes 7 days. No is preselected and goes back to the map; nobody has jumped
  - Yes plays the jump film; then: You have arrived in the Louzy system. Seven days have passed. Fuel: 61 tons. It stays until a click or a key, and the flight title reads Flying in the Louzy system
  - Jump again at Louzy: nothing flashes; the map says Not enough fuel to jump: you have 61 tons, Jump-1 needs 87. Refuel at a starport or scoop at a gas giant. A click or a key goes back to the flight
