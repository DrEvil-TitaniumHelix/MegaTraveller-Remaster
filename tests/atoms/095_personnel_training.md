# T095 Train an eligible Traveller

OPEN AT: The Efate starport, Personnel > Train open at Training Eligibility. Magellan has earned enough practice; carousing is the first course, and the Party Account will pay.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/095.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Magellan / carousing to buy the training [Enter]
  2. Read the training confirmation, then click Leave [Esc]
  3. Click Traveller 1 in the Commands bar to read Magellan's skills [F1]
EVALUATE:
  - The eligibility list names Magellan, the available skills and their prices
  - The confirmation says Magellan receives training in carousing, and the Party Account pays the displayed price
  - Magellan's carousing skill rises by one; the other skills are unchanged
