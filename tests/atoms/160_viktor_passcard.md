# T160 Defeat Viktor and take his passcard

OPEN AT: The last shot of the Boughene fight, north-east of the starport. Magellan is the only remaining Traveller; wounded Viktor is two squares to his right. The loaded rifle is ready.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/160.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Right-click Viktor, then click Fire [F in the menu]
  2. Walk to the square where Viktor fell [Right twice]
  3. Click Items, Take, then Magellan [I, Enter, Enter]
  4. Open Magellan's record [F1] to inspect the passcard
EVALUATE:
  - The shot defeats Viktor and the fight ends.
  - A passcard can be picked up from where he fell.
  - The passcard is in Magellan's pack and the rifle has used one round.
