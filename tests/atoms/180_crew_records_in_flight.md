OPEN AT: The Pilot station aboard the Interloper in free flight in the Efate system, with all five Travellers assigned to stations.
SETUP KEYS: Open snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/180.json.
ACTIONS:
  1. Click: Magellan's tile (first of the five crew tiles, top)   [F1]
  2. Click: each of the other four tiles   [F2, F3, F4, F5]
  3. Click: Close   [Esc]
EVALUATE:

  - Switching records keeps the ship in space
