# T125 Collect mustering-out benefits and cash

OPEN AT: A Marine mustering out after the first term: Benefits and Cash are offered, the remaining selections are shown, and the material-benefit table is open underneath.
SETUP KEYS: Follow T120 through the first-term skills and click Muster Out [Down, Enter]; stop at Benefits / Cash. Headless proof: acceptance/local/atoms/125.json. The window cannot load character-creation snapshots; live dice may differ.
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Benefits [Enter]
  2. Click Cash for the remaining selection [Right, Enter]
  3. Click the name box, type AtomBenefits and confirm [Enter]
  4. Click View Character [arrow keys, Enter], then AtomBenefits [arrow keys, Enter]
EVALUATE:
  - The Benefits choice awards the rolled benefit and leaves the next selection available
  - The Cash choice adds money to Credits
  - When the selections are spent the name box opens; the named Marine then appears in the pool
