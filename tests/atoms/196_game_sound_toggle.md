# T196 Turn game sound off and on

OPEN AT: The Game menu over the quiet Efate map, offering Restore, Quit, Sound off and Back. Sound is currently on.
SETUP KEYS: Open snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/196.json. Use normal system volume to hear the sound changes.
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Sound off [Up/Down to Sound off, Enter]
  2. Click the adjacent street square to the left [Left], then click Game [G]
  3. Click Sound on [Up/Down to Sound on, Enter]
  4. Click the street square to the right to return [Right], then click Game [G]; finish with Back [Esc]
EVALUATE:
  - Sound off returns to the map, and the next Game menu offers Sound on
  - Game effects and menu tones are silent while sound is off
  - Sound on restores game effects and menu tones; the next Game menu offers Sound off again
  - The party walks one square left and back without losing members or money
