# T074 The engineer station opens and closes

OPEN AT: The Interloper in space above Efate, the flight screen showing. The flight opens paused: PAUSED - press P to fly; click Pause (or press P) to fly.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/074.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Pause to fly (or press P), then Stations (or press S)
  2. Click the ENG icon in the station strip (or press V, Down four times, Enter); Repair answers a click
  3. Click the In-sys travel box at the top right (or press Esc)
EVALUATE:
  - V offers the stations to choose from
  - The fifth row opens engineering: repair component
  - Esc closes it to the flight screen
