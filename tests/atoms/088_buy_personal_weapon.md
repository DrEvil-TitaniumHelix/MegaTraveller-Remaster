# T088 Buy a weapon for a Traveller

OPEN AT: The Efate weapons store, Who will buy the items? showing. Magellan is already chosen to receive the weapon; the Party Account has 30,000 Cr.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/088.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Party Account [Down five times, Enter]
  2. Click the first weapon, 7mm revolver, once to buy it [Enter]
  3. Click Back to close the weapon list, the Weapons/Ammo/Grenades list and the store [Esc three times]
  4. Click Traveller 1 in the Commands bar to inspect Magellan's pack [F1]
EVALUATE:
  - The weapon list shows names and prices; buying the revolver reduces the Party Account by its displayed price
  - The shop acknowledges the purchase and stays open
  - Magellan has one 7mm revolver in his pack alongside his dagger; his own money is unchanged
