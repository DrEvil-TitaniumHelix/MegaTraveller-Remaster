# T105c Swap two Travellers in the marching order

OPEN AT: The ground screen on Efate, nothing open: the party stands as one figure beside a pond. Top right of the screen: the Marching order block (five coloured squares numbered 1 to 5, Magellan's purple square at 1) and left of it the button that reads Change order.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/105c.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click: Change order   [O]
  2. Click: square 1 (Magellan) > square 2 (Jurgen, orange, middle left)   [Enter, Down, Enter]
EVALUATE:
  - Squares 1 and 2 trade places: Jurgen's orange square moves to the top, Magellan's purple square goes below it.
  - The purple box says: Magellan and Jurgen swapped places. It goes after a few seconds.
  - The button reads Change order again.
