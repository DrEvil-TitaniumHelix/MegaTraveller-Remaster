# T115 Jump to Uakye and land there

OPEN AT: The jump map in the Efate system, Louzy, Uakye and Alell offered as destinations; Jump-2 and Navigation are already running.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/115.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Uakye [Down, Enter]
  2. After arrival, click the thrust digit to stop [keypad 5]; resume with [P] if PAUSED shows
  3. Click the right rotation arrow twice [Right twice] to point south, then click thrust up once [Up]
  4. Let the ship fly south until it reaches Uakye and Land appears; click Land [L]
EVALUATE:
  - Uakye is a selectable destination and the jump arrives in its system
  - The jump advances the date by seven days and uses half the fuel
  - The ship comes to rest at Uakye and offers Land
  - Land opens the ground map with Uakye named in the side panel
