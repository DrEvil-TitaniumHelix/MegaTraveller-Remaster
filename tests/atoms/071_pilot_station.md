# T071 The pilot station opens and closes

OPEN AT: The Interloper in space above Efate, the flight screen showing. The flight opens paused: PAUSED - press P to fly; click Pause (or press P) to fly.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/071.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Pause to fly (or press P), then Stations in the list at the right (or press S)
  2. Click the In-sys travel box at the top right (or press Esc): back to flight
EVALUATE:
  - S opens the pilot station
  - Esc closes it to the flight screen
