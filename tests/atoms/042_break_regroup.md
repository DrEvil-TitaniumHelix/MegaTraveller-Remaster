# T042 Break and Regroup outside a fight

OPEN AT: The Efate road after the ambush, nobody in sight, the party together
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/042.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Break on the bar above the map: the Party menu opens in the side panel (Break / Regroup / Back) after a moment
  2. Click Break in that menu
  3. Click Regroup on the bar, then Regroup in the menu
EVALUATE:
  - Break splits the party into five figures
  - Regroup brings them back as one
  - The bar's button opens the same menu as pressing P
