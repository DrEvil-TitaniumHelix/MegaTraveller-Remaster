# T201 Earn the last practice use and train Magellan

OPEN AT: The Efate starport services after 49 price enquiries. Magellan has 49 carousing practice uses, carousing 0 and no course bought; the Party Account still has $30000. One more enquiry earns training eligibility.
SETUP KEYS: Open snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/201.json. Keep Magellan selected; reopening the snapshot resets practice to 49 uses.
ACTIONS:
  1. Click: Personnel > Train > Party Account   [Esc after the result]
  2. Click: Shipyard > Buy Weapons > cancel   [Esc]
  3. Click: Personnel > Train > Party Account > Magellan / carousing > buy
  4. Open: Magellan's record   [Esc, F1]
EVALUATE:
  - Before the final enquiry, no carousing course is offered; a "no one has practised enough" reply is a correct refusal
  - One more price enquiry brings the total to 50 and offers Magellan / carousing for $10000
  - The enquiry costs no credits and does not increase carousing by itself
  - Buying the course raises carousing from 0 to 1 and leaves $20000 in the Party Account; the other skills stay unchanged
