# T177 Buy information at the library

OPEN AT: The library in Efate's gravatic city of Seara. The information list shows prices above short previews, starting with Efate's atmosphere; the Party Account is paying.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/177.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click the first price row, above the atmosphere preview, then confirm the purchase [from the list: + once, Enter]
  2. Read the full information page
  3. Close the page [Space]
EVALUATE:
  - The selected preview expands into a full information page about Efate's tainted atmosphere.
  - Closing the page deducts the displayed price from the Party Account.
  - The ground map returns with the party outside the library.
