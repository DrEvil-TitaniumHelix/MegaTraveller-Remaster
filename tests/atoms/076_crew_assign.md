# T076 Assign crew to the stations

OPEN AT: The Interloper in space above Efate, the flight screen showing. The flight opens paused: PAUSED - press P to fly; click Pause (or press P) to fly.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/076.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click Pause to fly (or press P), then Stations (or press S)
  2. Click Assign (or press A)
  3. Press Esc (the crew assignment draws no Back)
  4. Click the In-sys travel box at the top right (or press Esc)
EVALUATE:
  - A opens the crew assignment with each Traveller and station
  - Esc closes it
  - A second Esc returns to the flight screen
