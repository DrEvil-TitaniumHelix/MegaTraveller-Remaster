# T173 Choose a target and change fire mode

OPEN AT: The Efate ambush with the party spread out and Magellan selected. His loaded combat rifle appears in the right panel; he has not attacked.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/173.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Right-click a nearby thug and click Target [A while that menu is open]
  2. Cycle to another target [Tab]
  3. Right-click Magellan on the map and click Fire mode [Q while his menu is open]
EVALUATE:
  - Target marks the chosen thug without firing or using ammunition.
  - Tab moves the target marker to another visible target without firing.
  - Fire mode reports that Magellan switches to full auto and uses his Attack; his ammunition is unchanged.
