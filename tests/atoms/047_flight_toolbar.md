# T047 Flight controls by click

OPEN AT: The Interloper in space above Efate, the flight picture filling the game area, no Commands bar at the bottom. The flight opens paused: PAUSED - press P to fly.
SETUP KEYS: snapshot acceptance/local/atoms/047.json
ACTIONS:
  1. Click the thrust-up arrow in the panel (thr, right of rotate)
  2. Click the rotate-left arrow
  3. Click the thrust-down arrow
  4. Click the thrust digit between the arrows (hover: Stop - keypad 5): the ship brakes
EVALUATE:
  - Each arrow does what its key does (the thrust digit shows 1, the ship turns, the digit goes back)
  - Hover over an arrow or a row names it and its key
